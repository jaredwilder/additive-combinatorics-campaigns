## Positional and carry-lattice theorems

### CF-01 — bounded-residue zero detection

For `r=(r_0,...,r_{m-1}) in Z^m`, `|r_j|<=A`, `m>=2`, and

`Phi_B(r)=sum_j r_j B^j`,

`Phi_B(r)=0 => r=0` for every such `r` **iff `B>A`**.

Sharpness at `B<=A` is witnessed by `(-B,1,0,...)`.

### CF-02 — sharp balanced-cube injectivity

`Phi_B` is injective on `[-A,A]^m ∩ Z^m` **iff `B>2A`**. Therefore the least integer base is `2A+1`.

### CF-03 — carry-lattice kernel generation

The integer kernel of `Phi_B : Z^m -> Z` is generated by the adjacent carry vectors

`g_j = B e_j - e_{j+1}`, `j=0,...,m-2`.

Thus every positional collision is an integer combination of adjacent carries.

### CF-04 — relation localization

For coefficients `a=(a_1,...,a_n)`, digit alphabet `D`, and

`M=max{|sum_l a_l d_l| : d_l in D}`,

every global relation `sum_l a_l x_l=0` in the base-`B` digit language localizes coordinatewise whenever `B>M`.

### CF-05 / CF-06 — exact polynomial threshold and sparsity boundary

For every nonzero integer polynomial whose nonzero coefficients have absolute value at most `C`, evaluation at a positive integer `B` is guaranteed nonzero for the entire class **iff `B>C`**. Sharpness is `P(X)=X-B`.

Consequently sparsity, degree, or term count cannot lower the universal positional base below `2A+1` for signed digit alphabet `[-A,A]`: the sharp obstruction is already support two.

## Full-box extremality

### BOX-01 — output-span lower bound

If `x -> w·x` is injective on `[-A,A]^m`, then

`||w||_1 >= ((2A+1)^m-1)/(2A)`.

### BOX-02 — balanced positional weights are optimal

Weights `(1,q,...,q^{m-1})`, `q=2A+1`, attain the minimum possible output span and coefficient `l1` norm among integer linear scalarizations injective on the full cube.

### BOX-03 — rigidity at equality

Equality in the full-box output-span bound forces positional weights, up to coordinate permutation and independent sign changes.

The source marks this as plausibly classical canonical-numeration rigidity and recommends formal/literature checking; this release preserves that boundary.

### BOX-04 — heterogeneous mixed-radix extremality

For a rectangular product of digit alphabets of sizes `q_0,...,q_{m-1}`, mixed-radix weights

`1, q_0, q_0 q_1, ...`

attain the minimum contiguous output span `product_i q_i - 1`. The clean uniqueness formulation requires an ordering convention compatible with coordinate capacities.

## Sparse recovery and exact scalar encodings

### SP-01 — sparse moment uniqueness

A vector with support at most `2s` is determined by its first `2s` power moments

`sum_i z_i i^r`, `r=0,...,2s-1`.

The proof is the square Vandermonde inversion on the nonzero support.

### SP-02 — carry-free sparse moment encoding

Weights

`w_i = sum_{r=0}^{2s-1} i^r B^r`

with `B>4As m^{2s-1}` give an injective integer-linear scalar encoding of all `s`-sparse vectors in `[-A,A]^m`.

### SP-03 — finite-field sparse syndrome injectivity

For `p>max(m,2A)` and distinct `alpha_i in F_p`, the first `2s` Vandermonde syndromes

`sum_i x_i alpha_i^r`, `r=0,...,2s-1`,

uniquely determine every `s`-sparse integer vector `x in [-A,A]^m`.

### SP-04 — corrected `2s` lower-bound scope

At least `2s` linear field measurements are necessary to recover **every field-valued** `s`-sparse vector. This standard spark argument does **not** by itself prove `2s` optimal for a restricted bounded integer alphabet embedded in the field.

That correction is part of the theorem, not a footnote.

## Sparse subset / signed-state encoding

### BS-01 — binary sparse counting lower bound

If positive weights bounded by `W` uniquely encode every subset of `[m]` of size at most `s` by its sum, then

`sW+1 >= sum_{r=0}^s binom(m,r)`,

so `W=Omega_s(m^s)`.

### BS-02 / BS-03 — shifted B_s construction and optimal exponent

Given a cyclic `B_s` set `a_i mod N`, weights

`w_i=L+a_i`, `L=s(N-1)+1`,

uniquely encode every subset of size at most `s` by its integer sum. Combined with classical `B_s` constructions and BS-01, the optimal largest positive weight satisfies

`W(m,s)=Theta_s(m^s)`

for fixed `s`.

### SGN-01 — signed two-sparse counting lower bound

Any positive-weight scalar encoding every `x in {-1,0,1}^m` with support at most two requires

`max_i w_i >= m^2/2`.

### SGN-02 / SGN-03 — shifted Sidon construction and exact exponent

If `{a_i}` is a Sidon set in `[0,N]` and `L>4N`, then `w_i=L+a_i` uniquely encode all ternary signed states of support at most two. With classical quadratic-range Sidon constructions this gives optimal growth

`Theta(m^2)`.

The higher-sparsity ternary theorem `Theta_s(m^s)` and the general bounded-coefficient construction are released separately in `../signed-sparse-encodings/`.
