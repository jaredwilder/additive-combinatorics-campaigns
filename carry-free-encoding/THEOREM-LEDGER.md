## Proof cores

`CF-01`: take the least nonzero coordinate; divisibility by `B` contradicts `0<|r_t|<B`. Sharp witness `(-B,1,0,...)`.

`CF-03`: eliminate coordinates successively; kernel membership forces the current least coordinate to be divisible by `B`, so subtract a multiple of the adjacent carry vector.

`BOX-01`: the cube has `(2A+1)^m` points but the image lies in an interval containing at most `2A||w||_1+1` integers.

`BOX-03`: after shifting to digits `{0,...,q-1}`, equality in the count forces a bijection onto every integer from `0` to `q^m-1`; a gap/collision induction forces the positional weight sequence.

`SP-01`: restrict the moment equations to the nonzero support and invert the square Vandermonde matrix.

`SP-04`: any `r×m` field matrix with `r<2s` has a dependent set of at most `r+1≤2s` columns, yielding a nonzero kernel vector with support at most `2s`.

`BS-02`: the shift first forces equal subset cardinalities; reduction modulo `N`, padded to `s` terms, invokes `B_s` uniqueness.

`SGN-02`: the shift separates total signed charge; Sidon sums resolve equal-sign pairs and Sidon differences resolve opposite-sign ordered pairs.
